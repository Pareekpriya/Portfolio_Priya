Business Insights 360 — Atliq Hardware

An enterprise-grade, interactive business intelligence suite designed to monitor global operations, dissolve structural data silos, and track core KPIs across multiple departmental verticals.

📝 Project Case Study

  • SITUATION: Atliq Hardware faced major operational inefficiencies and blind spots because data was heavily siloed across separate departmental platforms.
  • TASK: To design and deploy a centralized, high-performance 360° business intelligence platform tracking key performance indicators across Finance, Sales, Marketing, and Supply Chain structures.
  • APPROACH: Ingested millions of rows of transaction records into an optimized star schema data model. Wrote complex DAX measures to calculate performance indicators, configured automated responsive tooltips for customer analytics, and built dynamic parameter selection toggles.
  • RESULT: Delivered an interactive C-Suite executive control application tracking metrics like Net Sales, Gross Margin %, Forecast Accuracy, and operational risks—instantly pointing out high-growth market opportunities.
